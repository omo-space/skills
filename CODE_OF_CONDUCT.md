# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Omo Skills welcoming, respectful,
and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or level of expertise.

## Expected behavior

Participants should be constructive, assume good faith, welcome different
perspectives, give and accept respectful feedback, and prioritize the safety
and privacy of learners, educators, and contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, personal
attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing private or
confidential information without permission.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and
other contributions that violate this code. They may take any action they deem
appropriate, including restricting participation, to protect the community.

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through the contact method
listed in the repository. Please include enough context to investigate and do
not share sensitive personal information unnecessarily.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant and applies in all project
spaces and whenever an individual is representing the project.
